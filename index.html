Projects

Thesis work
CFD Analysis of Heat Transfer in PCM-Based Compact Heat Storage
Master's Thesis · Jan 2025 – May 2026 · Supervisor: Dr. Shobhana Singh, Dept. of Mechanical Engineering, IIT Jodhpur
  • Ran transient CFD simulations of melting and solidification in spherical encapsulated PCM for thermal energy storage.
  • Handled meshing (structured/unstructured), a grid-independence study, boundary conditions, solver settings (pressure-based/coupled), and the finite volume method.
  • Post-processed temperature, liquid fraction, and non-dimensional numbers (Rayleigh, Nusselt); validated and verified results to improve simulation accuracy.
Mechanical Characterization of Nano-Filler Polyester Composites
Bachelor's Thesis · Sept 2019 – Apr 2020 · Supervisor: Dr. Akant Singh, Dept. of Mechanical Engineering, AKTU
  • Studied polyester composites reinforced with nano-fillers (silica and carbon nanotubes).
  • Ran tensile, flexural, and toughness tests, and analyzed how nano-filler concentration affects strength and toughness.
